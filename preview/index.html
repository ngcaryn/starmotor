Brand Milestones
2015
Starmotor founded in Shenzhen. First prototype unveiled at Beijing Auto Show with a mission to redefine the Chinese luxury vehicle.
2018
Series A closes at ¥2.8 billion. Gigafactory Phase 1 breaks ground in Wuhan with a target capacity of 200,000 vehicles per year.
2021
S7 sedans begin mass production. 50,000 pre-orders within 72 hours of reveal — a domestic record.
2024
Listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE: 688STAR). Revenue exceeds ¥120 billion. Expands to 8 overseas markets.
2026
Next-gen 800V solid-state platform launched. European showrooms open in London and Munich.

FAQ
How long does a standard service take? ▾
A standard scheduled maintenance visit takes approximately 2–3 hours. Our express lane is available for oil changes and tyre rotations at under 60 minutes.
Is my warranty valid at all Starmotor centres? ▾
Yes. All Starmotor-authorised service centres honour the full manufacturer warranty including the battery warranty on EV models.
Can I get a courtesy car? ▾
Courtesy vehicles are available at flagship service centres subject to availability. Please request one when booking your appointment.
Which overseas markets should we prioritize for ArcFox Alpha T5? ▾
High-potential starter markets usually include Norway, the Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Denmark, France, Spain, Portugal, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Israel, the UAE, Chile, Uruguay, Costa Rica, Thailand, and Vietnam. Prioritize countries that pair left-hand-drive compatibility with strong EV demand, supportive incentives, dense charging networks, healthy compact-SUV demand, and local distributor/service readiness.
